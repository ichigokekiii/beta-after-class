# After Class. Canva paste sheet

## How to use this

Open the Canva file After Class Pitch Deck. Stay at 10 slides.
Each slide lists the Canva box, the exact paste, and what to say.
Paste the exact words. Do not rewrite them.
Read Say this out loud. Do not paste that block.
Keep If they ask off the slide.
You are talking to a café owner, not an investor.
We asked 110 students. 143 people signed the waitlist. Two lists. Do not add them.
Lead with the freemium offer. First 100 customers sent to their shop are free advertising. After that, ₱5,000 a month to continue. Put both on slides 5 and 10. Do not put ₱5,000 on slide 1.
Do not promise how fast they hit 100, how many dates they will get, or how many people are on the app. The 100-customer cap is the offer, not a forecast.
Ask today is the partner list. Name, address, hours, photos. No contract.

## If they ask

**How many people in the first 30 days?** We have not launched. We will not guess tables. The first 100 customers at your shop are free. We are not promising how fast you hit that.

**How do you pick which café to suggest?** The midpoint between both students. Open at that hour. Public, daytime, around schools, places students already go. No bars or clubs.

**Is there chat in the app?** No. We set the place and time. The first conversation is in person.

**What about safety?** Daytime, public café. Location stays with us during the date. The other person only knows you are nearby, not your live pin. If something goes wrong, the two founders answer.

**Reserved table?** Optional. Our name on a table makes people walking by curious. We are not promising how many pairs yet.

**What happens after free?** First 100 customers sent to your shop are free advertising. After that, ₱5,000 a month to keep getting people and stay listed. You decide then. No charge today.

**What counts as a customer?** One student we send to your shop for a date. A date is two customers.

**Do I pay today?** No. List for free now. You only pay after 100 customers if you want to continue.

**October and November?** The app is free for everyone while we get into schools and prove dates in Manila. That is students. Your partner deal is separate. First 100 customers free, then ₱5,000 a month.

**No-shows?** They can cancel until 2 days before. Reminders go out before the date. Late is okay. Two no-shows, fewer people see them. Third time, they are out.

**Do I need a discount?** No. Only 5 of 110 students named a promo as the thing that would excite them.

**Which school?** Say the street that matches this shop. The counts live under slide 6.
